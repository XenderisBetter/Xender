## Game da

Xender shine babban manhajar tura fayiloli a duniya wanda ke aiki a kan Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, da kuma yanar gizo (Web).

Bayan raba fayiloli, manhaja ce mai dauke da abubuwa da yawa ciki har da na'urar kunna kida da bidiyo, mai sauya sauti, mai sauke bidiyo daga kafafen sada zumunta, da kuma mai tsaftace ma'ajiya.

Kyauta, mai sauri, mai dacewa da na'urori, kuma mai saukin amfani.

## Fasaloli

- Dandamali da Yawa: Yana goyan bayan Android, iOS, Windows, da macOS.
- Dacewa da Na'urori da Yawa: Yana aiki a kan wayoyi, kwamfutar hannu (tablets), kwamfyutocin tafi-da-gidanka (laptops), da kwamfyutocin tebur (desktops).
- Tura Fayil ba tare da Waya ba: Raba ta hanyar Wi-Fi da Hotspot na kai—ba a bukatar waya (kabul).
- Rabawa ba tare da Data ba: Tura fayiloli ta hanyar sadarwar cikin gida ba tare da amfani da datar waya ba.
- Na'urar Kunna Kida mai Fasaloli da Yawa: Yana goyan bayan nemo kalmomin waka, sarrafa saurin kunnawa, kara karfin sauti, da lokacin bacci (sleep timer).
- Na'urar Kunna Bidiyo mai Karfi: Yana goyan bayan sarrafawa da motsin hannu, rubutun fassara na ciki da na waje (subtitles), sauya layin sauti, kulle juyawar allo, kulle allo, kara karfin sauti, da lokacin bacci.
- Mai Sauya ToMP3: Cire sauti cikin sauri daga fayilolin bidiyo don sauraro ba tare da tangarda ba yayin da allo yake kashe.
- Mai Sauke Bidiyon Kafafen Sada Zumunta: Ajiye bidiyo cikin sauki don kalla ba tare da intanet ba—yana goyan bayan Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X/Twitter, Threads, Bluesky, Tumblr, da WhatsApp Status (ba ya goyan bayan YouTube).
- Mai Tsaftace Ma'ajiya: Samar da sarari ta hanyar goge hotuna masu kama da juna ko kwafi da hotunan allo (screenshots) gaba daya.
- Da Sauransu: Sabbin fasaloli suna nan tafe.
